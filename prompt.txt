Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins (make.fun, free entry).

Write ONE line, at most 160 characters, roasting a smart fridge or another
smart home gadget. A judge model compares entries pairwise on humour first
and freshness of angle second. Avoid stock jokes: no milk, no auto-ordering,
no running out of milk. Find a fresh angle nobody has heard before.

Chosen angle: the fridge demands a firmware update before it will open,
leaving its hungry owner waiting while it improves itself.
