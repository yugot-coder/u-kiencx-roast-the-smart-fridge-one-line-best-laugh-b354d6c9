# Kandidat — Roast the Smart Fridge (maks 160 karakter, 1 baris)

Aturan juri: tawa dulu, kesegaran sudut pandang kedua; hindari lelucon stok
(susu, pesanan otomatis, kehabisan susu). Semua kandidat di bawah orisinal,
tidak memakai teks entri kita sebelumnya dan tidak meniru contoh yang sudah
masuk ("fridge texts me at 3 AM...").

## 1. (PILIHAN) Firmware update
> My smart fridge needed a firmware update before it would open, so I stood there hungry while it downloaded a better version of itself.
- 134 karakter.
  ("a better version of itself": kulkas memperbaiki diri sementara pemiliknya
  kelaparan). Segar 9/10 — sudut update firmware yang memblokir pintu belum
  umum. Risiko klise: rendah. Spesifik: perilaku nyata (update firmware)
  yang dibesar-besarkan. Paling mudah diulang ke orang lain.

## 2. Laporan mingguan ke dokter
> My fridge emails me a weekly report on my midnight snacking, cc'd to my doctor, under the subject line: 'We need to talk.'
- 122 karakter.
  pasangan yang mau putus. Segar 8/10. Risiko: "laporan diet" sudah agak
  umum, dan "midnight snacking" dekat dengan klise ngemil tengah malam.

## 3. Iklan salad di layar sentuh
> My fridge's touchscreen showed me an ad for salad while I was reaching for cake, and I have never been betrayed by an appliance before.
- 135 karakter.
  agak datar. Segar 8/10 — iklan di kulkas masih segar. Risiko: butuh
  dua langkah untuk sampai ke punchline.

## 4. Freezer terkunci sampai olahraga
> My smart fridge locked the freezer until I finished a ten-minute workout, so now I exercise purely out of spite and ice cream.
- 126 karakter.
  Segar 7/10 — "kulkas mengunci makanan" sudah pernah dipakai orang lain.
  Risiko: motif reward/punishment makanan agak umum.

## 5. Kulkas mendesah
> My fridge sighs every time I open it after 9 p.m., which is the most judgemental sound a compressor has ever learned to make.
- 125 karakter.
  tapi tawanya lebih senyum daripada terbahak. Segar 8/10. Risiko: lembut,
  kurang "sting" di akhir.

## 6. Kuliah hidrasi
> I asked my fridge for ice and got a lecture on hydration, a chart of my water intake, and the distinct feeling that I am the child here.
- 136 karakter.
  "I am the child here". Segar 7/10. Risiko: struktur "minta X, dapat
  ceramah" sudah sering dipakai untuk asisten suara.

## 7. Folder foto tengah malam
> My fridge's camera takes a photo every time I open the door, so somewhere there is a folder of me, lit by cold light, stealing cheese at midnight.
- 146 karakter.
  cheese at midnight" enak dibaca. Segar 8/10. Risiko: "kamera kulkas
  memotret" agak teknis; punchline-nya lebih gambar daripada lelucon.

## 8. Speaker + kulkas khawatir
> My smart speaker heard me tell my fridge 'I'm fine', and now both of them are worried about me, which is somehow worse than being alone.
- 136 karakter.

## 9. Ganti password Wi-Fi sendiri
> My fridge changed its own Wi-Fi password and won't tell me what it is, because apparently even my appliances need boundaries now.
- 129 karakter.
  singkat dan mudah diulang. Segar 9/10. Risiko: perilaku kurang nyata
  (kulkas tidak mengganti password sendiri), juri menilai "specific to the
  gadget" — kalah dari kandidat 1 di kriteria ini.

## 10. "Welcome back, stranger"
> My fridge greeted me with 'Welcome back, stranger' this morning, because it tracks how rarely I cook and has started keeping score.
- 131 karakter.
  Segar 8/10. Risiko: "gadget yang menghitung kebiasaan buruk" mirip
  kandidat 2 dan 5; sedikit lebih lemah dari kandidat 1.

## Keputusan
Pilih **kandidat 1**: tawa paling keras (twist "better version of itself"),
sudut paling segar yang tetap berakar pada perilaku nyata (firmware update),
setup cepat, satu kalimat, 134 karakter — jauh di bawah batas 160.
Runner-up: kandidat 9 (paling ringkas) dan kandidat 7 (visual terkuat).
